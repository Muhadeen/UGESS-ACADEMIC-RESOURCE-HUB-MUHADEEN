About Me

Hello, Economics students! I am Muhadeen Mohammed, a dedicated Economics student at the University of Ghana and the elected President of the University of Ghana Economics Students' Society(UGESS). My journey in this department has shown me the academic struggles students face; finding the right study materials, accessing past questions, and collaborating effectively.

Through this platform, I continue to provide free, well-organized Economics resources for all students, ensuring that learning remains accessible, structured, and effective. What started as an initiative has now become a commitment to serve and improve the academic experience of every Economics student. My vision remains clear: to build a student society that actively supports your academic and professional growth while fostering unity, excellence, and opportunity. Together, we are building an Economics department where everyone thrives.

Economics is Life!

What You'll Find Here

This website serves as your one-stop resource center for Economics course materials:

  • Lecture slides for all Economics courses
  • Past examination questions with sample answers where available
  • Recommended textbooks and journal articles
  • Study guides and summary notes
  • Tutorial materials and practice problems

All resources are organized by year level, semester, and course code for seamless navigation, ensuring you can quickly find exactly what you need for your studies.

My Vision & Plans

As UGESS President, I will:

  • Expand and improve this online Economics resource hub.
  • Facilitate study groups and peer mentorship programs.
  • Enhance faculty-student engagement for better academic support.
  • Organize practical workshops to prepare students for the job market.
  • Improve access to past questions, lecture slides, and textbooks to make learning easier for all.
